03 / About

Learning in public.

I am a student at Wuhan University, turning coursework and experiments into cleaner repositories with useful documentation, focused scopes, and evidence that the work actually runs.

I am most interested in the boundary between understanding a system and making it useful: tracing an operating system path, testing an AI workflow for unsafe assumptions, or reducing a vision experiment to a reproducible baseline.

The goal is a body of work that reads as both a learning record and a technical portfolio.
